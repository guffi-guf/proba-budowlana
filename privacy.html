2. What information we collect

  • Enquiries. If you use the contact form or email or call us, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), the type of project and anything you tell us about it, such as your address and photos.
  • Customers. If you hire us, we also keep the details we need to do the work and get paid: the site address, quotes, invoices and payment records.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, the server that hosts this site records your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in standard security logs.

The contact form on this website does not send your message to our server. It opens a ready-written email in your own email app, and you choose whether to send it. We only receive it when you press send.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only where needed with:

  • our website hosting provider (Hostinger) and email provider;

Where a provider is outside the UK, we make sure there are appropriate safeguards in place for your information.

When that map loads, Google may receive your IP address and may store or read information on your device. See Google’s privacy policy for how it uses this information.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information, in some cases;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using it, in some cases;
  • object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • receive the information you gave us in a portable format, where it applies;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email dominika@dmrenovations.net. We normally reply within one month.

8. Keeping your information safe

This website is a simple static site with no accounts or passwords. We use secure hosting and restrict who can access the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
